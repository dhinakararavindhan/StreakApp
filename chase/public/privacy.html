Privacy

Chase 300 is a free, fan-made game. It does not use accounts, ads or tracking cookies.

What we store on your device

Your streak, best scores, level progress, display name and a random anonymous device ID are kept in your browser's local storage.

What we store on our server

  • When you post a score: your anonymous device ID, the display name you typed, the day, your drafted players and the resulting score.
  • When you play a friend challenge: the same details, tied to the challenge code.
  • If you turn on streak reminders: your browser's push-subscription address and your timezone offset, used only to send reminders.

We do not collect your real name, email, location or contacts. Your IP address may appear in standard server logs and is used only for abuse prevention.

Your choices

Turn reminders off in the app at any time, or clear this site's data in your browser to remove your local data and device ID. To ask for server-side entries to be deleted, contact the address below with your display name and date played.

Not affiliated
