Privacy

Chase 300 is a free, fan-made game. It does not use accounts, ads or tracking cookies.

What we store on your device

Your streak, best scores, level progress, display name and a random anonymous device ID are kept in your browser's local storage.

What we store on our server

  • Scores and challenges: when you post a score or play a friend challenge, we store an anonymous device ID, the display name you typed, the day, your drafted players and the result.
  • Reminders: if you turn on streak reminders, your browser's push-subscription address and your timezone offset.
  • Anonymous usage stats: simple events such as "game started" or "level cleared", with a few numbers (mode, level, score), a one-way hashed version of your device ID, your country (from the connection), whether you use a phone or computer, your operating system and whether the app is installed. We do not store your IP address, your name, your exact location or your raw device ID in these stats, and we keep them for up to 180 days.
  • Cloud backup (only if you turn it on): a copy of your game progress (levels, streak, stats, saved matches, and your optional profile if you added one) stored under a private recovery code. We keep only a one-way hash of the code, so we cannot see or recover it. Anyone who holds the code can restore or overwrite that backup, so keep it private. You can remove the backup at any time in the app.
  • Optional profile (only if you choose to add it): an age band, your state and a favourite team city, stored with your explicit consent. We use these only to understand our audience in groups, for example "30% of players are 18 to 24", and may share group numbers (never individuals) with advertisers or sponsors. Groups smaller than 5 are never shown.

Children

If you tell us you are under 18, we do not store any profile details and we switch off usage stats for you. The game itself stays fully playable.

Your choices

In the app, open Privacy & data on the home screen to switch anonymous usage stats off, add, change or delete your optional profile, or delete all your data from our server (profile, leaderboard scores, challenge entries, reminder subscription, cloud backup and usage stats). You can also turn reminders off in the app, or clear this site's data in your browser to remove local data and your device ID.

We do not collect your real name, email, phone number, contacts or precise location, and we do not sell personal data.

Not affiliated

Fan-made, free and unofficial. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any league, board, team or player. Team and player names are used for identification only. Ratings are fan estimates.
